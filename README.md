# 华语 L1–5 · Mandarin basics

**▶ Open the app: https://boci0.github.io/cn_basic/**

A study sheet for the 49 compulsory characters of lessons 1–5, plus the lesson vocab.

- Tap any card for its sound and an animated stroke order.
- **Easy → hard** groups the characters by stroke count.
- **Assessment** practises the real Assessment 1 format, level by level:
  1. Listen drill: hear a character, pick its meaning.
  2. Write drill: see the meaning, write the character.
  3. Mock test: a lettered English list, 10 characters read aloud, then 10 to write in 15 minutes, scored out of 15%.

  Each level unlocks after 8/10 on both parts of the previous level's mock test.

It works offline once it has loaded, and you can install it to your home screen from the browser menu.

## Run it on your own computer

Double-click `Start.bat` (needs Python). It opens the app at http://127.0.0.1:8137/.
